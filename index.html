Services

Four Pillars of Support

Programs designed to be accessible, culturally sensitive, and community-focused.

Pillar 1

Enrollment Assistance

We help people navigate and enroll in benefits they already qualify for.

  • Medi-Cal and Medicare enrollment
  • CalFresh applications and renewals
  • Eligibility requirements and documents
  • Connections to local food resources
Check Your Eligibility
Pillar 2

Education Outreach

Free workshops brought to shelters, schools, and community centers.

  • Preventive healthcare and early detection
  • Digestive and colon health
  • Nutrition and healthy lifestyle habits
  • Women's health and healthcare navigation
  • Clinic referral list and info booklet
Pillar 3

Digital Literacy Program

Closing the gap when barriers to care happen online first.

  • Step-by-step Medi-Cal and CalFresh online applications
  • Scam awareness for healthcare and benefits fraud
  • Telehealth setup and virtual appointment literacy
Pillar 4

Community Transportation

Approved benefits mean nothing if someone can't physically get there.

  • LA Metro LIFE Program enrollment support
  • $11 Metro taxi vouchers via CCSI
  • Navigator Model: accompany or coordinate rides to SSA, Medi-Cal, interviews, and medical visits
